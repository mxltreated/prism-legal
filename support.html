Support & TestFlight Beta FAQ

Need help with Prism Research Reader or want to report a TestFlight issue? Reach out anytime at mxltreatment@gmail.com.

How do I send TestFlight beta feedback?

You can take a screenshot while using Prism and tap Share Beta Feedback in TestFlight, use the Send Beta Feedback option in the TestFlight app, or open Settings → About Prism → Send Feedback & Support inside Prism to email mxltreatment@gmail.com with your app version included.

Do I need an account to use Prism?

No. Prism works completely in Local-Only Mode by default. Your saved papers, offline PDFs, topics, and on-device personalization stay on your iPhone or iPad.

How does optional Sign in with Apple & iCloud Sync work?

If you sign in with Apple in Onboarding or Settings, Prism requests no name or email address—only an anonymous Apple user identifier stored in your device Keychain. Your followed topics and saved paper IDs sync across your devices via your personal Apple iCloud Key-Value Store.

How does sharing papers between Prism users work?

When you share a paper from Prism, the link contains only its public scholarly identifier (such as a DOI, arXiv ID, or PubMed ID) and an optional short title — never cached PDFs, abstracts, or tracking data. If the recipient has Prism installed, tapping the link opens the paper inside Prism; otherwise, Safari displays a static preview page with a link to the official source.

How do I delete my data or account?

Open Settings → Storage & Personalization to clear cached PDFs, search history, taste signals, or your saved library. If you signed in with Apple, tap Delete Account in Settings → Account & Sync to erase synced iCloud data (and optionally all local data). To revoke Apple authorization, open iOS Settings → Apple Account → Sign in with Apple.
