Privacy Policy — Prism Research Reader

Effective Date: November 25, 2026
Developer / Operator: Nasir Cooley
Contact: mxltreatment@gmail.com

Prism ("Prism Research Reader"), operated by Nasir Cooley, is designed around local-first privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Prism handles local storage, direct requests to scholarly indexes, and optional Sign in with Apple sync.

1. Local-First Privacy Architecture

Prism is designed to operate in Local-Only Mode by default without requiring an account. Your saved papers, offline PDFs, followed topics, recent search history, and reading taste signals are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData and UserDefaults frameworks. Prism contains no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or cross-app tracking, and your data is never sold or shared for advertising.

2. On-Device Personalization & Offline Storage

Feed ranking (Faces, Latest, and Important Reads) and search recommendations are computed entirely on your device using Apple's NaturalLanguage framework and local TF-IDF scoring based on your reading interactions (opens, reading dwell time, likes/dislikes, and bookmarks). Downloaded Open Access PDFs are stored inside your device's application sandbox under SavedPDFs/ and marked with isExcludedFromBackup so cached PDFs do not bloat unencrypted backups.

3. Direct Requests to Scholarly Indexes & Publishers

When you refresh feeds, search, or open/download a paper, your device makes direct HTTPS requests to public scholarly APIs — OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar (Allen Institute for AI), arXiv, PubMed / NCBI NLM E-utilities, and Crossref — as well as external publisher or repository hosts when loading PDFs or web pages in Safari (SFSafariViewController). These third-party services receive standard HTTP request metadata (your IP address, query terms or topic filters, and the PrismResearchApp/1.0 User-Agent header with operator contact mxltreatment@gmail.com for API polite-pool compliance) governed by their respective privacy policies. Prism never attaches your Apple ID, name, or personal email address to scholarly search or feed queries.

4. Optional Sign in with Apple & iCloud Sync

If you choose to use Sign in with Apple in Onboarding or Settings, Prism requests no name or email scopes—it only receives an anonymous, app-scoped Apple user identifier. That identifier is stored exclusively in the device-only iOS Keychain (kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly) to enable syncing your followed topics and saved paper identifiers across your devices via your personal Apple iCloud Key-Value Store (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore). Prism periodically checks Apple credential revocation state (ASAuthorizationAppleIDProvider) and automatically signs out if you revoke access; you can also revoke Sign in with Apple at any time in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Sign in with Apple.

5. App-to-App Paper Sharing & Deep Links

Shared Prism links encode only canonical public scholarly identifiers (DOI, arXiv ID, PubMed ID, OpenAlex ID, or Semantic Scholar ID) and an optional short display title—never your Apple ID, device identifier, reading history, taste signals, or tracking or referral parameters. Sharing uses Apple's native iOS Share Sheet; once shared, transmission is governed by the messaging or destination app you choose. Opening a shared link inside Prism causes the recipient's device to look up the paper locally first and otherwise query public scholarly APIs directly (subject to Section 3). Opening a link on Prism's static web fallback page in Safari runs client-side with zero cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts and links directly to the official scholarly source.

6. Data Retention, Deletion Controls & Your Privacy Rights

You have full control over your data at all times in Settings → Storage & Personalization and Account & Sync: you can clear recent search history and cached network responses, reset your reading taste profile, clear cached offline PDFs, clear your saved library, sign out to return to Local-Only Mode, or permanently delete your account and all cloud/local data. Under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), these in-app controls provide immediate self-service access, rectification, and erasure of your personal data.

7. Children's Privacy & Contact

Prism is a general-audience academic research reader and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK, or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). For privacy questions or requests, contact mxltreatment@gmail.com.
